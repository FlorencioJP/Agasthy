# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want AGASTHY to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate, including when you disagree.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Assume good intent and ask before concluding otherwise.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation or discrimination in any form.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Insults, personal or political attacks, and trolling.
- Publishing someone's private information without explicit permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and chat) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behaviour is harmful. Report concerns privately to the maintainers. Contact details will be published here when the project's public repository is created. Reports will be handled confidentially.
